Hello! I am Benedict Saunders, a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Warwick, specialising in materials design and property prediction using DFT and machine learning. My PhD focussed on the exploration of the phase space of complex oxides using DFT, and applying machine methods to predict the properties of doped graphene and other defective 2D materials.
